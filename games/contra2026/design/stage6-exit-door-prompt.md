最终提示词

> Use case: stylized-concept. Generate a production sprite atlas for a single animated industrial blast door in a high-detail painted side-scrolling action game. Input image is a style and placement reference for a tall factory exit; isolate and completely redesign just the door components. Exactly two isolated parts in one image, orthographic straight-on elevation, no perspective skew, both aligned vertically, transparent gutters and exterior: (left two thirds) a large freestanding heavy inverted-U blast-door frame, tall overall width-to-height about 1:2, with absolutely empty transparent interior opening, two straight narrow parallel jambs, and a thick dark mechanical top lintel; NO bottom connecting beam or threshold. (right third) one independent tall sliding door LEAF, straight rectangular silhouette, full opaque surface, width-to-height about 1:4, roughly the same height as the frame opening. Its right edge is the inner locking edge of the left leaf and should be straight and mechanical; it will be mirrored for the second leaf. Do not join pieces or overlap their bounding boxes. Rich crisp game texture like a painted pixel-art remaster, near-black blue-gray gunmetal, warm orange furnace bounce light catching edges, desaturated steel highlights, heat-stained oxide, worn brushed metal, shallow recessed armor plates, load-bearing bolts, dark inset seams, scratches, side hydraulic guide channels and cable conduits, subtle red-brown enamel wear. Bold readable silhouette and major details at gameplay size; detailed but restrained. Keep the frame slim (jambs roughly 14% each of its outer width; lintel roughly 8% of height). A dark recessed space in the lintel to later place a separate amber/green status light, no baked glow. Leaf should have broad graphite interlocking structural plates and one restrained short black/yellow hazard strip near its base. Do not draw any part of the surrounding factory, ground, text, labels, extra objects, interior texture between the jambs, environment, shadows outside the silhouettes or lighting spill. Genuine alpha transparency around each isolated part and entirely through the frame opening.
